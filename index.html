About Us

Spice Garden started in 2012 as a small tea and snacks shop. Over the years, with the support of our regular customers, it has grown into a full family restaurant.

We use fresh vegetables, ground masalas and good quality oil. Nothing is kept for the next day. Whether you come for a quick lunch or a family dinner, we try to make sure you leave happy.

Open daily: 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM
